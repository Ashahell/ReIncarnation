## Routing matrix (`engine/fx/route.{h,c}`, new)

Owners per unit (−1 none, 0–3 sections, 4 master for comp only).
`ri_route_assign` enforces radio (steal returns previous owner);
bad unit/owner and dist-or-pcf-on-master rejected (−2, unchanged).
`ri_route_section_mask` feeds the render loop.

## Engine wiring (`engine.{h,c}`)

- One Dist + PCF + Comp voice each, applied to the owning section bus
  in Dist→PCF→Comp series (t52b: A-solo moves, B-solo bit-identical).
- Per-section pan (linear wings, exact centre detent 64 → (1,1)) +
  post-insert mono send, square law (t52c/d).
- Shared delay on a caller-owned line (`ri_engine_set_delay`; NULL =
  dry default; attach preserves knobs, forces wet — send topology has
  no dry path), tempo field (default 140, clamped 20–500) driving
  `ri_fxdelay_resync`, stereo return with its own pan.
- Master comp = new `ri_fxcomp_render_linked` (one detector on
  max(|L|,|R|), same gain both channels, GR tracked); GR readable via
  `ri_engine_comp_gr` (t52e).
- Neutral renders bit-identical (t37 + all engine goldens green
  unmodified; t52a/f pin unity + chunk-agnosticism with FX engaged).
