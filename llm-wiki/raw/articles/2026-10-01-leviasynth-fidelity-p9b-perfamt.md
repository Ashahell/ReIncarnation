## Method findings (portable)

- **A clamp can make a law vacuous.** The analog filter's keytrack defaults to ×4, so the analog cutoff is `12000 × 4` and sits *on* the 20 kHz clamp. A full +8 octave amount is therefore a silent no-op at the default: the first "APAT moves the analog cutoff" law asserted a difference that could not exist, and the test passed only because the mirror law (an exact 40 Hz twin) was independently correct. Any "an amount moves X" law must start from a base where the amount cannot be absorbed by a clamp.
- **Exact twins beat thresholds wherever a reference value exists.** `10240 = 40 · 2^8` makes the 8-octave scale an exact equality, so an octave-error mutant dies with no tolerance in the law at all. The same trick pins the bipolar read: soft with `+1` and hard with `−1` are the *same* number, so the law is bit-identity rather than a near-equality.
- **The neutral pivot may be unreachable, and that is a design fact, not a test bug.** With integer velocities, `velb` is never exactly 0 (that needs velocity 63.5), so "the amount does nothing at mid velocity" cannot be asserted at all. Unipolar-vs-bipolar is pinned instead by the exact symmetry laws plus the VCA silence floor.
- **A second render path is a second contract, and it hides a real defect.** The mono sum was never exercised by the P9a laws, so it kept a stale `vel01` for a whole slice. The P9b mono twins failed for a reason that had nothing to do with the amounts; fixing the refresh (a shared `levi_sig_refresh`) was the honest repair, and it is safe only because the mono sum has no production caller.
- **Unipolar signals have no symmetry twin.** The velocity law (soft with `+` = hard with `−`) has no pressure analogue, because `+1` at full pressure is not `−1` at no pressure. The pressure laws are: no pressure is neutral (bit-identical), full pressure moves, and the negative end at full pressure is the 40 Hz reference.
- **"Cancelled" and "silent" are different states in a matrix.** A route into the VCA scales the amp by `1 + x`, so a mod-env curve that drives the envelope source to 0 reads as *neutral*, not as silence. The audible law on that side is the doubling law; the cancellation law is a bit-identity against the "no curve" twin.
- **A panel slot that prints its raw value cannot distinguish two formatters at the default.** Both the amount group and the plain knob text print "0" for the default 64, so a text law that only reads the default position is blind to a dropped branch. Text laws must move the value off its default.
- Test traps: the op envelope level starts near its 0..1 ceiling, so a hard note's +50 % depth mostly clamps and the measured ratio was 1.02 — the law now sets a lower ENV LVL so the comparison is off the clamp. A helper that builds "same-age twins" cannot change the velocity of a voice that is already sounding the same key (the allocator steals the voice), so the symmetry laws build their two sets by hand. And a velocity-0 twin that is also given an amount is a *cancelling* twin, not an identical one.
- Mutation driver: the stereo and mono copies of each amount block are textually identical, so a single-line mutant cannot be unique in that file. `mut.sh` now accepts a literal `\n` in the OLD/NEW fields, which lets a mutant span the block through the following `voice_chain` line and be unique.
