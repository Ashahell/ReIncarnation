## Production changes

- Wrapper stores BEATS (was overwritten with 0.75 every render);
  retarget slews the tap (≤1/64th per sample, chunk-agnostic) instead of
  jumping; `ri_fxdelay_retarget` added, `sync` stays immediate.
- Static delay pool retired: `RiFXCreateDelay(buf, cap)` (caller sizes for
  the 32-triplet/20-BPM worst case) + `RiFXDestroy` (slot reuse, null +
  double-destroy safe); plain Create(DELAY) fails closed; MAX cap dropped.
- Comp follower re-derives per render (was stale init-time 48 kHz).
- Clamps widened to D-e normative (bpm 20–500, beats ≤ 32).
- t1_fx §7 rewritten to create/destroy/reuse (deliberate).
