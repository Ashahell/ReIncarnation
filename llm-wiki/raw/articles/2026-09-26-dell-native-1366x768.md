# Dell E6320 reference box now boots at the panel's native 1366x768

- Source: ReIncarnation lane work, 2026-09-26 (Dell reachable again after the USB-stick backup)
- Collected: 2026-09-26
- Published: 2026-09-26
- Cross-post: Vulkan4AROS llm-wiki `raw/articles/2026-09-26-aros-screen-resolution-vesa-native-panel.md`

## What changed

- The Dell ran at 1024x768 (`vesa=1024x768` in the GRUB "AROS Default" entry).
- Its GPU (8086:0126, Sandy Bridge HD 3000) is not supported by AROS `IntelGMA`, so `vesagfx.hidd` drives the screen with the single VBE mode the bootstrap sets.
- The bootstrap's `findMode` takes an exact `vesa=` match, else the closest area, so the change cannot make the box unbootable.
- A new default entry "AROS Native Panel (1366x768x32)" was added. It is an exact copy of the old entry with `vesa=1366x768x32`; the old entry is the next menu item, and the original config is backed up as `SYS:boot/grub/grub.cfg.pre-1366`.
- The owner rebooted the box; agent session 25 reconnected, and the capture measured 1366x768.

## Consequences for the GUI work

- The Dell screen is 1366 px wide: the Transport (842 px at 1x) and the 303/808/909 sections (~732 px at 1x) fit at 1x, and 1.5x fits for single sections.
- Screen-dependent evidence recorded at 1024x768 (the Task-12 panel-909 lock) was measured at the old width. Re-measure before comparing new captures against it.
- Higher than 1366x768 is not available on the internal panel through VESA. Beyond that would need native Sandy Bridge support in IntelGMA (a large driver job, a follow-up).
