## What
- **909 param section** (production change, the one the pins demanded).
  `engine/dsp/params.c` gains the 909 block after the 808 section;
  `RI_CTL_909_TUNE/LEVEL/DECAY/FLAMRES` = `0x0900..0x0903` (`rb909.h`,
  panels.c order) + `rb909_set_param(struct RB909Voice*, uint32_t ctl_id,
  uint8_t value)` decl in `rb909.h`. TUNE writes the engine tune byte
  directly (knob domain IS the engine domain — `rb909_pitch_mult` and
  `rb909_layer_weight` read it raw, so no curve table); LEVEL/DECAY/
  FLAMRES are documented placeholder breaks (no engine gain field —
  mixer owns level; decay is baked in layers with CR/RD scale
  tune-derived; flam delay is trigger-time scheduler state).
- **Five per-TC pins** (t22/t23 CHECK-macro pattern, self-contained
  fixtures, `return fails != 0`):
  - `t24_909xfade` (TC-2.4.1 + Tune wiring): rendered tune sweep 0..127
    on a 2-layer sine fixture, worst adjacent-bin RMS step ≤ 1 dB, with
    the worst step inside the P-13 feather neighborhoods (63|64 ± 4)
    reported separately; white-box `rb909_layer_weight` ramp points
    (1.0 inside [lo+4,hi-4], 0.875 one step in, 0.0 past the far edge,
    monotone); panel Tune knob 0/64/127 → engine byte.
  - `t24_909accent` (TC-2.4.2): acc1 ratio in [1.124, 1.178]
    (1.15 ±0.2 dB); flam onset 1680±240 @48 kHz, matched-age peak ratio
    0.75±0.10; acc2 on CH == acc1 within 2% RMS.
  - `t24_909quirk` (TC-2.4.3): CR + RD accent bit-identical no-op
    (maxdiff == 0.0), each voice direct.
  - `t24_909retrig` (TC-2.4.4): mid-ring retrigger bit-exact vs fresh
    (maxdiff == 0.0).
  - `t24_909swap` (TC-2.4.5): `RI_909_BUSY` immediately after trigger
    AND mid-ring (voice still active), rc 0 after drain to idle,
