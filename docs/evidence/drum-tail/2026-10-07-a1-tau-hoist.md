## The A1 fix and its prize

`rb909_env_tau()` computes `1.2 × decay_scale(id, tune)` once per trigger
and per TUNE-knob move (the knob writes `tune` directly — a trigger-only
refresh goes stale); `decay_env()` reads the cache. Bit-exact by
construction (identical inputs, identical bits): every 808/909 golden and
unit test green with unchanged pins; the three song hashes unchanged
(demo `238113a7962c112f`, ZB `26eb9a9911173c20`, KN `13dc42d73d85998b`).

- Host prize: crash/ride solo 9693 → 7086 ns/block (−27%), all
  `drum_bench` controls still OK.
- Gated law `t177_drum_envtau`: trigger path and knob path bit-identical
  at non-default tune; cached constant follows both writers. Mutants
  killed: knob refresh dropped; helper ignoring tune.
- Dell A,B,B,A on Zombie Nation (A = `521ab7e`, B = `6536353`, all full,
  xruns+0):

A,A spread: 909 avg 31/31, max 87/94, stg_total 2160/2161. Against it:

## Verdict (owner-confirmed 2026-10-07): REVERT

Per §0 ("a fix without a measured prize is not [a valid outcome]") and
gate GA1 (host AND Dell improvement on the named outlier — Dell half not
met), the fix is reverted in full (code + `t177` + gate). What stands:

- "909 spikes" was **polyphony, not a defect** (A2 verdict): r ≥ 0.95 on
  all four fits; the 808's larger max/avg ratio at r = 0.999 says the
  same.
- Crash/ride cost ~2.1× per voice-sample by design (audible envelope);
  the exact cut recovered its pow2 half on the host but nothing
  attributable on target. Any further saving there changes the sound and
  needs the owner-approved trade of §9 item 3.
- Instruments kept: per-block drum counters (`t175`), `drum_bench`, the
  Dell `(us, active)` ring + dump, TAB `fn/fb/fr/fl/mui` probe (`t176`).
- Lane lessons: bare `SONG=` loads nothing and wedges a modal requester
  remote input cannot dismiss (owner clicked it by hand); the window title
  gains the song leaf, so closes must use the full title; `RIAPP.LOG`
  rolls at 256 KB — fetch `.LOG.1` too; never build two trees into one
  `/tmp/ri/*` dir (stale-object roulette, twice in one day).
