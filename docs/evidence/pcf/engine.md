## SVF engine shape (candidate)

Textbook Chamberlin two-pole (12 dB), per sample in this order:

```
low  += f * band
high  = in - low - damp * band     (damp = 1/Q)
band += f * high
out   = low | band | high           (mode 0/1/2)
```

with `f = 2·sin(π·fc/sr)` via `ri_sin`, `fc` clamped to [10, sr/6]
(Chamberlin bound, Appendix B), Q clamped to [0.7, 8]. State init zero;
denormals snapped to exact zero inside ±1e-30 (no FTZ flags). DC gain 1
(`t1_fx` §2: settled 0.5-tone rms 0.500000); 10 kHz at base 1000 cut to
inaudibility; double-render bit-identical (D1).
