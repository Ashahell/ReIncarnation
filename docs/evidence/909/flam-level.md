# 909 flam hit level (OPEN with E0 default)

**Status:** E0 default adopted 2026-09-25, pattern-model slice; OPEN
until measured.

**Rule:** flam hits play at **low** level. A flam is a separate hit
type entered with one click (p. 30); high level requires the second
click, which flam mode does not offer. Flam hits therefore carry no
`RI_EVFLAG_ACCENT`.

**Source:** E1 (pp. 30–31) defines flam as its own hit type; the level
is the E0 inference above.

**How it closes:** export a flam hit from ReBirth 2.0.1; compare the
peak of both flam hits against low and high hits of the same
instrument.
