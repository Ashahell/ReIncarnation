# Tap level (909 and drums)

DECIDED 2026-09-26 (owner, E1): tapped-in hits record at LOW velocity.
Emphasis comes from tapping AC (accent has its own tap key, the minus
key). Owner rationale: the manual (p. 32 and p. 226) gives accent its own
tap key and says nothing about tapped hits playing hard, so taps record
Low; High would make accent pointless for tapped hits, and "follow
accent" mixes the two rows the manual keeps separate. A step can be
changed to High afterwards by clicking it.

Scope note: taps need the instrument canvases (a later slice); this pins
the law only. Supersedes the G6a "909 low E0".
