ADDENDUM, same day, after the song ended (23:36) and the app was relaunched for
the owner at 23:41:57 (rc=0, fresh instance, playing=2903 stopped=15,
build=386abb8, SONG load then TR PLAY). The addendum exists because two claims
made above are WRONG and the numbers that refute them arrived after the file was
written. They are corrected here, not rewritten above.

A. THE SONG IS ~4 min 20 s, NOT ~49 s. The EV `t` field and `stg: playing` are
   the same counter, in AUDIO BUFFERS (~187.5/s at 48 kHz / 256 frames) -- not
   milliseconds. Three independent checks:

   two runs, same song from bar 0:
       playing froze at 48 864   and TR STOP logged at t = 48 873   (2026-10-22)
       playing froze at 48 866   and TR STOP logged at t = 48 881   (2026-10-24)
   48 866 buffers / 187.5 = 260.6 s. The two numbers agreeing across runs also
   refutes item 9's "the earlier stop was an already-advanced cursor": it is
   the SAME full-song length, measured twice.

   the stopped counter is the clock that settles it:
       after the stop, `stopped=38265` at hb 23:39:26
       stop at 23:36:02 -> 204 s of stopped buffers -> 38265/204 = 187.6/s ✓
       (if TR STOP had really been at 48.9 s wall time, `stopped` would have had
       ~416 s to accumulate and would read ~78 000, not 38 265)

   the bar arithmetic agrees:
       151 bars x 16 sixteenths x (60/140/4 s per sixteenth = 0.1071 s) = 258.9 s
       258.9 s x 187.5 buffers/s = 48 550 buffers, ~ one bar of lead-in -> ~48 870

   during play the counter grows 2913 -> 8790 in 31 s = 189.6/s -- the buffer
   rate, NOT 1000/s, which is why it is not a millisecond clock.

   CONSEQUENCE: "the song is ~49 s" was a units error (a buffer count read as
   milliseconds), repeated in the 2026-10-23 re-stage entry. A relaunch buys
   ~4:20 of song, so a 60-second relaunch cycle was unnecessary churn and would
   have cut a listening round short.

B. THE PLAY/STOP PAIRS ARE A PRESS, NOT THE END-OF-SONG RULE. This run logged
   exactly ONE `TR STOP` (ev 24) and no pairs at all after the song ended. A
   Play press at the end produces `TR PLAY` then `TR STOP` within a few ms
   (song_poll_end fires on the next poll), so a long run of pairs at ~37 ev/s is
   repeated presses or a stuck key -- as the lane note already says, a held raw
   key auto-repeats. The 19 575-line EV log from the previous session is
   therefore an input artefact, not the app restarting itself.
